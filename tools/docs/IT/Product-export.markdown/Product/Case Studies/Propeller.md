# How We Built *Propeller*, the all-in-one SaaS Platform Powering the Future of Boat Rentals


## **The Challenge**

Propeller approached us with an ambitious vision:

***"We want to build the operating system for boat rental businesses - a single platform that simplifies everything from fleet management and customer communication to bookings and payments."***


\
The challenge? The boating industry was stuck with fragmented tools and manual processes. Fleet owners juggled spreadsheets, emails, and booking platforms that weren't built for them. Propeller needed:

* A scalable, multi-tenant application
* Real-time booking management for fleets and charters
* Payments solution
* Integrated tool to maintain relationship with customers
* A clean, intuitive UI accessible to both tech-savvy operators and small business owners
* Infrastructure ready to support operators on different markets all around the world

## The Result

### ==In less than **12 months**==, Propeller went from an idea to a polished SaaS product that's changing how the boating industry operates.


**Key outcomes:**

* **Seamless booking & fleet management** in one intuitive dashboard
* **Reduced operational overhead** for boat rental businesses 
* **Scalable architecture** ready for international expansion
* Integrated CRM system centralizing all customer data
* Automated notifications & reminders keeping both customers and crews informed 
* Flexible pricing setup allowing operators to manage dynamic rates, discounts, and seasonal packages (dynamic pricing)
* **Boat Maintenance flow** enabling proactive service tracking, reducing downtime, and extending fleet longevity


### *Propeller is now positioning itself as **the all-in-one operating system for boat rental companies**, and we're proud to be the technology partner powering that transformation.*
