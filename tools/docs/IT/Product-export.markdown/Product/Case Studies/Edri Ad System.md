# **E.ON Drive Ad System - Turning EV Charging Screens into a Smart Advertising Network**

### **Client**

E.ON Drive

### **Industry**

eMobility, Digital Advertising

### **Services**

Product Management, UX Design, Web Application Development, Go-to-Market Strategy


## **Project Overview**

As electric mobility grows across Europe, E.ON Drive identified a unique opportunity: transforming their fast-charging stations into a **premium digital advertising network**. However, existing workflows were manual - relying on spreadsheets, emails, and time-consuming coordination with suppliers.

E.ON Drive partnered with Iguana to design and develop an **end-to-end Ad Reservation System** that automates campaign planning, booking, and creatives management - making the ordering process faster, more transparent, and scalable.


## **The Challenge**

Before the project, EDRI's advertising operations involved:

* Manual tracking of charger locations, availability, and prices
* No unified view for campaigns planning or reporting
* Difficult coordination between marketing, operations, and suppliers
* Limited scalability for international markets\n

The goal was clear: **Create a self-service platform for advertisers** and an **operationally efficient admin portal** for EDRI - without overcomplicating the experience.


## **The Solution: E.ON Drive Ad System**

We designed and delivered a **two-sided web application**:

* **Client Portal** – where advertisers can plan, book, and manage their ad campaigns
* **Admin Portal** – where EDRI manages locations, prices, orders, and campaign approvals

The system connects both sides with intuitive UX and convenient notifications system, significantly reducing manual effort.


## **Key Features & Product Decisions**

### **Search & Discovery**

Clients can browse all available charging station locations on an interactive map, helping them identify the most strategic ad placements. They can also filter by address, city, names of charging locations.

*Why it matters:* Instead of searching through static lists, a map-based UI helps clients instantly visualize campaign reach and simplifies location comparison.


### **Automated Order Creation**

Once locations are selected, the system automatically:

* Calculates pricing based on location capacity and ad units
* Prevents overbooking with real-time availability checks

*Why it matters:* This eliminates manual validation and ensures operational consistency between client-side bookings and supplier uploads.


### **Creatives Management**

Clients can upload creative files directly within the system. Each charger type requires specific dimensions - the platform automatically validates uploaded files and displays upload deadlines.

*Why it matters:* Built-in creative validation reduces human errors, communication ping-pongs and prevents costly campaign delays.


### **Smart Notifications & Workflow Automation**

Automated email and in-app notifications keep both Admins and Clients informed throughout the order lifecycle:

* Campaign confirmation and rejection
* Pre-launch creative upload reminders
* Creative submission, acceptance, or rejection
* "Ending soon" and "Ended" campaign alerts with upselling CTA\n

*Why it matters:* The system ensures clear communication and no missed deadlines, improving both service quality and operational control.


### **Admin Efficiency Tools**

The Admin Portal provides all necessary management views:

* Orders Overview: one dashboard for all campaigns
* Creatives Overview: central panel to track uploads/removals to suppliers systems
* Internal notes, filters, and copy-to-clipboard tools for efficient daily work

  \

*Why it matters:* What used to take hours of manual coordination is now managed in minutes.


## **Results & Impact**

* **Streamlined operations** - fully digital campaign booking replacing manual spreadsheets
* **Transparency** - Admins and Clients share one unified source of truth
* **Scalability** - even though we delivered only MVP for Czech and Danish markets, the system is fully ready for rollout across multiple European and worldwide markets anytime it's needed
