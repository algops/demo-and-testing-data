# New Client Discovery

When meeting a new client, often they expect from us different outputs. It is important to understand what it is that the client wants to achieve and offer them either a free first help, or a paid one. Different services require different effort.


### 1. Draft concept & ballpark costs - ==FREE==


1. **Reason**: Rough estimation of a problem they need a solution for. They are ok with a range and do not need a precise cost. If they need a detailed estimation & concept, they need to pay for 3 - Defined Concept & Estimation

   a. **Output**: Draft concept of the solution and give a price range (example 1\~2 million CZK)

   b. **Example**: EDRI cz, Family Business Week, EDRI CMS

   c. **How**: 1 discovery meeting w/ client, couple internal meetings, 1 validation meeting w/ client, 1 draft solution w/ ballpark estimations

   d. **Cost**: This free phase should not take more than 20-25 hours internally!

### 2. Analysis - ==PAID==


1. **Reason:** Analysis of their current solution

   
   1. **Output**: Due Diligence (technical and product)
   2. **example**: FreewayCamper, Lightverse (phase 1)
   3. **How: Meetings, Questionnaire - follow this** [**helptool**](https://iguana.wiki/doc/1-analysis-sveIjfmUnt)

### 3. Defined Concept & Estimations - ==PAID==


1. **Reason:** Estimation of a problem they don't have a solution for - 

   
   1. **Output**: MVP Definition (concept) + Estimation of work (Cost) + Estimated Timeline (Roadmap)
   2. **Example**: Lightverse (phase 2), Family Business Week, Kind of carivio (but paid just 1/4 of the price so got less)
   3. **How: In person meetings with clients, Internal PRDs, Draft specs, Roadmap - follow this** [**helptool**](https://iguana.wiki/doc/2-concept-estimations-gJi54p5edM)
   4. **Cost:** it varies depending on the complexity of the product the client is looking to solve. Can range between 100-300 hours. Cost must be provided prior to starting.

### 4. Ad-hoc consultation - ==PAID==


1. **Reason:** Consultation in a specific domain (Architecture, Product, Design)

   
   1. **Output:** Leading certain aspect of their discovery/development workflow 
   2. **Example:** FerdiGo


Different clients may combine more reasons in 1 request. However it must be kept in mind, that each of those steps requires different type of effort and different type of discovery.

We should be able to know how much each of those steps will take us, before we start to conduct it - we don't want to work for free.

To make sure we have some guidelines to follow to complete each of those steps from a Product perspective, you can find here 3 documents that will explain how to achieve the output in each of those 3 cases.
