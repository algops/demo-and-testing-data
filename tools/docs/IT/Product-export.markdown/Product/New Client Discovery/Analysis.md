# Questionnaire

This questionnaire is helpful to discover a completely new client, about whom you don't have too many information. It covers basics such as business model & requirements, and goes more in depth in case you need to be able to understand the business to a point you need to go more in depth & provide estimations.

❗The questionnaire focuses on a Product evaluation and does not cover the Technical part of it! If you need to produce a more technical analysis, that shall be provided in a different form and be lead by a different role.


The questionnaire allows you to


1. Analyze their solution (in case they have one already)
2. Analyze their concept (in case they have an idea, but not a digital solution)
3. Provide feedback on the solidity of their idea/solution (Due diligence product basics)
4. Gather more insights to start drafting MVP definition

## Project Overview


* Describe briefly what your company does

### Primary Goals

* What are the main objectives you want to achieve with this solution?

### Problems to be Solved

* What specific challenges or issues is this solution intended to address?



---

## Project Scope


### Current Systems

* Are there any existing systems or software that this project needs to integrate with or replace?

### Target Audience

* Who are the users of the application?
* What do they do?
* Why would people be interested in you?
* How did you validate that your users are facing challenges?



---

## Team


### Applicable for clients which have or cooperate with a Product&Dev team

* What is your team composed of?
* What roles in the company are covered/not covered?
* Who is your product person, if any? Who is responsible for design?
* Who gathers and verifies those business requirements?
* Who leads the development? 
* Are developers involved in discussing those business requirements?
* Describe the process to transform a business requirement into a fully developed feature.



---

## Business Requirements


### Vision

* What are you trying to achieve with this solution?
* Goals and ambitions within the next 3 years?
* Will the project need to be scaled? Current markets and future markets?

### Business Processes

* What is the business model?
* What does a purchase mean for you? How is it processed?
* How can this solution help you to make money?
* What specific business processes or workflows will this solution support?

### Success Criteria

* How will you measure the success of this project?

### Budget

* What is your budget for this project?

### Timeline

* What are your key milestones and deadlines?
