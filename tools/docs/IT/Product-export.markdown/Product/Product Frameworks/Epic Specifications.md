### Journeys & Stories

**User(s) Journey**: a journey is the flow (with it's variations), it's short and describes what actions need to be performed from beginning to the end

**User(s) Story** : a story is very short, in 1st or 3rd person, and only tackles 1 real life example at a time. Takes into account a real scenario of a real customer, gives it a name, and says what that customer wants to do and how the story ends. Stories can be positive (end with expected conversion) or negative (user drops).

**NOTE: Include at least 1 story per user type (customers, operators, admins).**
