# Product Discovery workflow

Product discovery is the part that precedes the development of bigger epics. This is the time when somebody's requests turn into ideas, get brainstormed, solutioned, designed, specified and eventually handed over to the development team. 

To ensure that we have a standard process in Iguana that is the same across all projects that require Product, please follow the below flowchart as a help tool, to ensure you have done all it takes before you hand over more complex epic to developers. Note: the order of the steps might vary a little from project to project, the point is to have produced the below before you hand it all over to the devs.

By sticking to the process, we ensure to produce valuable features that meet the Client needs, add value to the business, avoid wasting developers capacity and ensure maximum efficiency while cooperating between Product Owners, Designers, Developers and QA.
